## Model limits

- The corpus is a word list, so bigrams never cross a space and punctuation is almost absent.
- Distance is measured from each finger's home key; travel between consecutive keys is not modelled.
- Shift and AltGr count as extra keystrokes but not as finger presses.
- The space after a word is assumed to be pressed with the right thumb (`word_end_space_hand`).
- Layouts without native accents use a dead key on the Quote key, like v1.
